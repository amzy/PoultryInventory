Poultry Inventory Management

OvalOasis Poultry Inventory helps poultry farm teams manage flock information, daily production records, feed, water, grit, suppliers, flock performance, standards, and operational reports in one place.

What you can do

  • Maintain flock and supplier information.
  • Record and review daily poultry production and inventory data.
  • Track feed, water, grit, eggs, laying performance, and flock age.
  • Compare flock performance with BV300 lifecycle benchmarks.
  • View reports and manage application data.
