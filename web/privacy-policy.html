Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OvalOasis Poultry Inventory (the “Application”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Application and its website at https://www.ovaloasis.in/.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: When you create or access an account, we may receive your name, email address, profile picture, and authentication information provided by your selected sign-in method, including Google Sign-In.
  • Farm and inventory information: Information you enter into the Application, such as flock details, daily production records, feed, water, grit, suppliers, contacts, reports, and other poultry-management information.
  • Profile information: If you choose to add a profile photo, the Application may access the photo you select or a photo you take with your device camera.
  • Device contacts: If you choose the contact-import feature, the Application requests access to your device contact picker so you can select contacts and add their names and phone numbers to your supplier list. The feature is user initiated.
  • Technical information: Basic technical and security information may be processed by Firebase and other service providers to authenticate users, store application data, and operate the service.

2. Google user data

If you use Google Sign-In, the Application receives the Google account information that Google makes available for authentication, which may include your name, email address, and profile picture. We use this information to create or authenticate your Application account, identify your account, display your profile information, and provide the requested Application services.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising. Google user data is used only for the functionality described in this Privacy Policy and the Application.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the Application, authenticate users, store and synchronize poultry-management records, maintain supplier and contact information that you choose to add, provide reports and performance features, respond to support requests, and improve reliability and security.

4. How information is stored

The Application uses Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage. Information is associated with your account and is protected using the security controls provided by the service configuration. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Application and comply with applicable obligations, unless you request deletion or another retention period is required.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that operate infrastructure required for the Application, such as Firebase, or when disclosure is required by law, legal process, or to protect the security and rights of users and the service.

6. Device permissions

The Application may request camera, photo-library, and contact-picker access when you choose features that require them. You can deny these permissions, although the corresponding optional feature may not work. We do not access your device contacts merely because the Application is installed; contact access is initiated when you use the contact-import feature.

7. Your choices and rights

You can review or update information you provide in the Application. You can stop using optional device features by denying the corresponding device permission. You may request access to or deletion of your account information by contacting us.

8. Children's privacy

The Application is intended for farm and business users and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Application, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
