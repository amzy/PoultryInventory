# OvalOasis Web OAuth Verification

Public URLs:
- Homepage: https://www.ovaloasis.in/
- Privacy Policy: https://www.ovaloasis.in/privacy-policy.html
- Terms & Conditions: https://www.ovaloasis.in/terms-and-conditions.html

The homepage is intentionally public and does not require authentication. The Flutter application is started by the “Open Poultry Inventory” button.

For Google OAuth verification, verify `ovaloasis.in` in Google Search Console and add the domain to Google Cloud authorized domains. Configure the same public homepage and privacy policy URLs on the Google OAuth branding/consent screen.
